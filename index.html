The Quest

Resonatia is a choose-your-path visual novel with d20 dice mechanics. Your decisions, your statistics, and the roll of a twenty-sided die shape your fate.

Starting Out

  1. Take the brief personality quiz. Your answers determine your class — Wizard, Cleric, Barbarian, or Bard.
  2. Choose your species for stat bonuses.
  3. Pick a gender and appearance for your character sprite.
  4. Step into the kingdom of Jurnaheim.

Stat Checks & the d20

When a choice is marked with a stat and Difficulty Class — for example INT DC 13 — the game rolls a 20-sided die.

  • Total = d20 + your stat modifier
  • Total ≥ DC → Success
  • Natural 20 → Critical Success (bonus reward)
  • Natural 1 → Critical Failure (extra penalty)
  • Choices marked “Free” always succeed.

Your Stats

STRStrength — physical might. Barbarian’s specialty.
INTIntelligence — arcane knowledge. Wizard’s specialty.
CHACharisma — influence & divine grace. Cleric and Bard.
DEXDexterity — dodge, accuracy, light weapons.

Combat

Battles are turn-based. Your party and the enemies act in alternating turns. On your turn, choose an action:

QStrike — Basic weapon attack.
EAbility — Class skill. Costs Stamina.
RDefend — Halve incoming damage this round.
IInspect — View your stats and the enemy roster.
FWait — Skip your turn, recover Stamina.

When several foes stand against you, the game asks you to click which enemy to target after you choose an action.

Health & Stamina

  • HP (Hit Points) — when this reaches zero, your character falls.
  • SP (Stamina Points) — required to cast Abilities. Regenerates 8 SP per round.
  • Heavy armor on bosses reduces damage you deal — rely on Abilities for big hits.

Tips for Survival

  • Pre-battle dialogue choices grant buffs. Pick the option that matches your class’s strength.
  • Don’t burn all your SP early — bosses need your most powerful skills.
  • Defend if your HP is low and a heavy enemy turn is coming.
  • Read the Combat Log — it tells you the exact roll, modifiers, and DC of every action.

Controls

  • Click the dialogue box to advance text or skip a typewriter line.
  • Click an enemy sprite to target it in combat.
  • Press H at any time to reopen this codex.
  • Press Esc to close any open menu.
